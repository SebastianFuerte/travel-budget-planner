# Privacy Policy — Travel Budget Planner

**Last updated:** April 13, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Travel Budget Planner ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. **All data you enter stays exclusively on your device.** We do not have servers, we do not create user accounts, and we have no ability to access, view, or collect your personal information.

---

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to:

- Your name, email address, or any contact information
- Your travel plans, destinations, or budget details
- Photos or documents you add to the app
- Your location
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or behavioral data

---

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information **locally on your device only**, using your phone's built-in storage (AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Trip details (destination, dates, travelers, budget) | Core app functionality |
| Budget breakdowns and calculations | Core app functionality |
| Travel documents (photos, PDFs, notes) | Document Vault feature |
| Visa and entry requirement notes | Entry Requirements feature |
| Trip timeline events | Timeline feature |
| App preferences and settings | User experience |

**You can delete all of this data at any time by uninstalling the App.** Uninstalling the App permanently removes all stored data from your device.

---

## 4. The Only External Service We Use

The App makes one type of network request: fetching **currency exchange rates** from [exchangerate-api.com](https://www.exchangerate-api.com) to convert budget amounts between currencies.

- **What is sent:** Only the base currency code (e.g., `USD`, `EUR`, `COP`) — nothing personal
- **What is received:** A list of exchange rates
- **When it happens:** When you view or calculate a budget that involves currency conversion
- **Offline fallback:** If no internet is available, the App uses cached rates from your last session

exchangerate-api.com's privacy policy: [https://www.exchangerate-api.com/docs/free-unregistered-endpoint](https://www.exchangerate-api.com/docs/free-unregistered-endpoint)

---

## 5. Documents and Photos

When you add a document or photo to the App's Document Vault:

- The file is **copied to your device's local storage** for the App to access
- The original file in your photo library or file system is not modified
- The copy is **never uploaded to any server or cloud service**
- We have no access to any documents or photos you store in the App

---

## 6. No Analytics or Tracking

The App does not use any analytics SDK, crash reporting service, or third-party tracking library. There are no cookies, pixels, or fingerprinting mechanisms.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data leaves the device, there is nothing to collect.

---

## 8. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a strong screen lock.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Because we collect no personal data, any changes will only affect how we describe the App's functionality.
