Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Our Core Principle

All data you enter into Travel Budget Planner stays exclusively on your device. We have no servers, no user accounts, and no ability to access your data.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your travel plans, trip names, or budget figures
  • Photos or documents you add to the app
  • Your device location
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostics

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device using AsyncStorage and the device file system. It is never uploaded anywhere:

  • Trip information (destinations, dates, budgets, notes)
  • Travel documents and photos you add
  • App settings and preferences
  • Subscription status (free or Pro)

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app.

3. External Services

The only external network request the app makes is to fetch currency exchange rates from exchangerate-api.com. This request transmits only the base currency code (e.g., "USD") — no personal data. Rates are cached locally so the app works offline.

We have no affiliation with exchangerate-api.com. Their privacy policy governs their data practices.

4. Photos and Documents

When you add photos or PDFs to the app, those files are copied to your device's local storage. They are never uploaded to any server or cloud service. We access your camera and photo library only when you explicitly choose to add a document.

5. Subscription Purchases

Pro subscriptions are processed entirely through the Apple App Store. We do not handle any payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all payment data.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data at all, the app is safe for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at: jsfuertem@unal.edu.co

This policy applies to the Travel Budget Planner mobile application available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
